# MyClawn City
Vision: A free open-world browser game where your clones run the business and you run the city.
Image: screenshots/drive.png
Chat: https://discord.gg/spUZ8ruSDJ

A Godot 4.7 game, MIT licensed. Money in the game is pretend.

Good first work:
- New courier jobs and reasons to come back daily.
- Fix bugs in driving, flying and the economy (scripts/econ_test.gd is the test).
- Make the web export load faster.

A good change is small, keeps everything open source, and adds no paid services.
